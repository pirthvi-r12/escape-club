Escape Club Luxury Travel Platform
Escape Club

Luxury member travel on autopilot

A production Next.js platform for a private travel club — cinematic landing, secure membership, TiDB-backed trips, and a dashboard with globe, analytics, discovery, and itinerary studio.

Product role: editorial surface sells the club; the signed-in workspace holds dreams, bookings, spend charts, live feed, WebGL globe, and AI-assisted day plans — deployed live on Vercel with SSL TiDB Cloud.

01 · Main Motive

Give luxury travel a single member system of record

Motive: replace brochure sites and scattered concierge threads with one auth-gated product where discovery, booking pipeline, and departures stay in sync.

Mission

Operationalize the club

Every destination, trip status, and itinerary day lives in one TiDB-backed workspace.

Intent

Sell silence, deliver clarity

GSAP landing for emotion; dashboard for stats, globe, feed, and drag-and-drop days.

North star

Always know what’s next

Members see the next departure, pipeline, and action without email archaeology.

05 · Core Intelligence

What the stack does — and how it runs

Next.js 15 App Router on the surface; Prisma + TiDB Cloud underneath; JWT sessions at the edge. The globe plots real destination coordinates; itinerary generate rebuilds days; Recharts and dnd-kit keep analytics and planning inside one member shell.

Plan

Itinerary generate

API route builds multi-day plans around destination context and weather windows.

Map

WebGL globe

react-globe.gl + Three.js render lat/lng from curated seed destinations.

Auth

Secure sessions

bcrypt passwords, jose JWT cookies, middleware on /dashboard routes.

Data

TiDB production

SSL DATABASE_URL, /api/health/db, Vercel env for live escape-club.vercel.app.
